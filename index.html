Privacy Policy

Unclaimed — Find Your Money

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Unclaimed mobile app ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. The app helps you reach your U.S. state's official unclaimed-property website so you can search for and claim money that may belong to you. We built the app to be private by design.

Information we do NOT collect

The app does not ask for, and the developer does not receive or store, any personal information about you. We have no account system. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, Social Security number, or financial details. Any personal information you enter to search or file a claim is entered only on your state's own official government website — never inside this app, and never sent to us.

Information stored on your device

To make the app work, a small amount of data is saved locally on your device only:

  • The U.S. state you choose.
  • A flag indicating you have completed the welcome screens.

This data stays on your device, is not transmitted to us, and is removed if you uninstall the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, Google's mobile advertising service. To serve ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share certain data, as described in Google's own disclosures:

  • Approximate location (estimated from your device's IP address).
  • App interaction data (such as when an ad is shown or tapped).
  • Diagnostic and crash information.
  • Device and advertising identifiers (for example the Android Advertising ID).

This data is handled by Google under its own privacy policy, which you can read at policies.google.com/privacy. All data collected by the app is encrypted in transit (TLS).

You can reset or limit ad personalization at any time in your device settings: Settings → Privacy → Ads (Android), where you can reset or delete your Advertising ID.

Children's privacy

The app is a general finance utility intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Not affiliated with any government

Unclaimed is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any state government agency, the National Association of Unclaimed Property Administrators (NAUPA), or any other official body. Searching for and claiming unclaimed property through your state is always free.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
